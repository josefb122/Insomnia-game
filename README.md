# SleepEase - Evidence-Based Insomnia Relief

A relaxing Android app designed to help you fall back asleep when you wake up in the middle of the night from stress and insomnia.

## Features

### 4-7-8 Breathing Exercise
- **Evidence-based technique** by Dr. Andrew Weil
- Clinically proven to reduce anxiety and promote sleep
- Visual breathing guide with calming animations
- Inhale: 4 seconds, Hold: 7 seconds, Exhale: 8 seconds

### Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)
- **Evidence-based tension release** method
- Guided through 14 muscle groups
- Proven to reduce physical stress and promote deep relaxation
- Tense for 5 seconds, relax for 10 seconds

### Calming Visualization
- Peaceful animated night sky with warm-colored stars
- Gentle wave animations
- **Guided imagery** - proven sleep aid technique
- No stimulating content

### Mindful Counting
- **Meditative counting** with visual mandalas
- Slow-paced (3.5 seconds per count)
- Helps quiet racing thoughts
- Rotating calming patterns

## Design for Sleep

### Warm Color Palette
- **NO BLUE LIGHT** - completely removed to avoid sleep disruption
- Warm amber, orange, and golden tones
- Deep, dark backgrounds to minimize light exposure
- Colors chosen specifically to promote melatonin production

### Optimized for Samsung Fold 7
- Resolution: 2184 x 1968 pixels
- Landscape orientation for inner 8-inch screen
- High-resolution animations and graphics
- Large, easy-to-read text

## Scientific Basis

All techniques are evidence-based:
- **4-7-8 Breathing**: Published research on anxiety reduction
- **Progressive Muscle Relaxation**: Decades of clinical use for insomnia
- **Guided Imagery**: Proven to reduce cognitive arousal before sleep
- **Mindfulness Meditation**: Shown to improve sleep quality
- **Warm Light Spectrum**: Avoids blue light's melatonin-suppressing effects

## Installation

1. Download `SleepEase.apk`
2. Enable "Install from Unknown Sources" on your Samsung Fold 7
3. Install the APK
4. Open SleepEase when you wake up in the middle of the night

## Usage Tips

- Use in a dark room
- Dim your phone's brightness further if needed
- Try different techniques to see what works best for you
- Don't force sleep - let the techniques guide you naturally
- If one technique doesn't work, try another

## Privacy

- No internet connection required
- No data collection
- No ads
- Completely offline

